Boids with Predator


        Craig Reynolds' classic Boids model simulates flocking using Separation, Alignment, and Cohesion.

        This simulation introduces a predator (@). The flock gains a fourth rule: Flee from the predator. The predator simply chases the nearest boid.

        Observe how the flock organically splits to avoid the predator and seamlessly reforms behind it, a beautiful emergent evasion tactic seen in nature.
