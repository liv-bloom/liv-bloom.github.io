Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction (ASCII)


        A cellular automaton model of the Belousov-Zhabotinsky oscillating chemical reaction.

        This reaction produces striking, self-organizing spiral waves out of a homogeneous mixture. 
        It mathematically models how three simulated chemicals (A, B, and C) react and diffuse over time. 
        Instead of settling into a flat equilibrium, they constantly cycle, creating one of nature's most beautiful chemical clocks.
