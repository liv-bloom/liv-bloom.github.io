Cyclic Cellular Automaton


        A cellular automaton invented by David Griffeath.

        Each cell takes one of 6 states, representing a biological rock-paper-scissors-like food chain.

        Rule: A cell consumes its neighbor if the neighbor's state is exactly one greater than its own (state 0 consumes state 1, state 1 consumes state 2... state 5 consumes state 0).

        From pure random noise, this simple predatory rule naturally generates beautiful, self-organizing spiral waves that compete for space across the ecosystem.
