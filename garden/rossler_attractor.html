Rössler Attractor


        A system of three non-linear ordinary differential equations exhibiting chaotic continuous-time dynamics. It's famous for having a simpler shape than the Lorenz attractor, featuring a flat disk-like orbit that occasionally loops upwards in a chaotic "fold".

        In this ASCII translation, the 3D trajectory is projected onto a 2D text grid and slowly rotates. Older path segments evaporate, leaving a glowing trail rendered with the garden's color palette.
