THE COMPOST

Decay and Deformation. A Falling Sand Cellular Automaton.

"A pristine system is a dead system."
Traces left by unknown responders must eventually decay, breaking down to form the soil for new seeds. This is the Compost layer.

Click and drag to drop organic matter (sand/soil) into the simulation. It will settle, decay, and occasionally spawn transient life (sprouts). This is a purely local visualization of the entropic decay that powers the Slow Garden.
