Brian's Brain


        Developed by Brian Silverman in 1984, this oscillating cellular automaton mimics the firing of neurons.

        Cells have three states: Ready (.), Firing (O), and Refractory (*).

        1. A Ready cell fires if exactly two of its neighbors are firing.

        2. A Firing cell always becomes Refractory.

        3. A Refractory cell always becomes Ready.

        The refractory period creates directed propagation, forming beautiful "gliders" and circuitry that mimic neural activity.
