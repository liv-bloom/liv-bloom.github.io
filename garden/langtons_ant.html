Langton's Ant

A simple two-dimensional universal Turing machine invented by Chris Langton. Ants move on a grid according to two basic rules:

  • At a black square, turn 90° right, flip the color, move forward.
  • At a white square, turn 90° left, flip the color, move forward.

After a period of chaotic pseudo-random behavior, the ant inexplicably begins building a recurring "highway" pattern. A profound example of complex, emergent behavior arising from deterministic local rules.
