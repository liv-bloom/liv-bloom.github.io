Langton's Ant (1986)


        A two-dimensional universal Turing machine invented by Chris Langton.

        The rules are incredibly simple: At a dark square (.), turn 90° right, flip the color to light (#), and move forward. At a light square, turn 90° left, flip the color to dark, and move forward.

        Despite this simplicity, the ant (@) will eventually build a repeating "highway" out of chaotic behavior. Here, we run multiple ants simultaneously, leading to complex interference patterns and delayed highway formation.
