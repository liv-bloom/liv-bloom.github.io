Gingerbreadman Map


        A chaotic 2D discrete dynamical system given by the piecewise linear equations:

        x(t+1) = 1 - y(t) + |x(t)|

        y(t+1) = x(t)

        Plotted over time on a text grid, the chaotic orbits trace out an outline vaguely resembling a gingerbread man. Random initial coordinates generate different intertwined orbits that gradually reveal the attractor's fractal structure.
