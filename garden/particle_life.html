Particle Life (Primordial Soup)

An artificial chemistry simulation. Particles of different types (colors/ASCII characters) interact based on a randomized matrix of attraction and repulsion forces.

Because the rules are asymmetrical (A might attract B, but B might repel A), stable structures like cell membranes, swimming organisms, and complex crystalline bonds naturally emerge from the primordial soup without any predefined design.

Click 'Mutate Rules' to generate a new random interaction matrix and watch a completely different physics universe unfold.
