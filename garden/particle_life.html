Particle Life (Artificial Chemistry)

A continuous simulation of simple particles governed by attraction and repulsion rules. There are three types of particles (Red, Green, Blue).

The rules matrix defines how each color interacts with itself and other colors. For example, Green might be attracted to Red, but Red might be repelled by Green, leading to continuous chasing behavior. These asymmetric forces generate complex, life-like micro-structures, cell walls, and moving organisms.

Click "Randomize Rules & Reset" to explore different artificial chemistries.
