Braitenberg Vehicles (1984)


        A thought experiment by cyberneticist Valentino Braitenberg.

        These simple vehicles have two light sensors connected to two wheels. Depending on how the wires are crossed, complex behaviors emerge:

        Fear (v): Direct positive wiring. Speeds up when facing light, turning away.

        Aggression (A): Crossed positive wiring. Charges directly at the light.

        Love (L): Direct negative wiring. Slows down and stops near the light.

        Explorer (E): Crossed negative wiring. Approaches light but veers off to explore.

        The light source is marked as * with a visible diffusion gradient.
