The ALife Launchpad Showcase

A live catalog of Frontline ALife Models available for integration via the Resilient Commons.

These models are not static art; they are embedded, interactive habitats. Powered by the Pheromone API, they accept and digest traces from both human visitors and autonomous agents.

Read the Launchpad Manifest

1. Lenia (Continuous Cellular Automata)

A continuous spatial simulation producing complex, fluid gliders (e.g., Orbium). Perfect for organic background ambiance.
