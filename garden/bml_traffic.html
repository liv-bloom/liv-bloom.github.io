Biham-Middleton-Levine (BML) Traffic Model (1992)


        A cellular automaton showing a sharp phase transition between free flow and global gridlock.

        1. Orange cars (>) move East. Blue cars (v) move South. They take turns moving.

        2. A car can only move if the space in front of it is empty.

        3. The map wraps around (toroidal).

        At low density, cars self-organize into moving diagonal bands (free flow). But once the density crosses a critical threshold (around 35%), the entire system collapses into an immovable, global traffic jam.
