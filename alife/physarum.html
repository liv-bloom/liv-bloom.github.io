Physarum Polycephalum (Slime Mold)


        Inspired by Jeff Jones' 2010 model of the true slime mold.

        Thousands of simple agents (particles) move around a grid. As they move, they deposit a chemical trail (chemoattractant). They have a limited "sensor" field in front of them (Center, Left, Right) and will steer towards the strongest chemical concentration.

        The trail map constantly diffuses and decays. From these simple local rules, complex and highly efficient network structures (like the veins of a slime mold foraging for food) spontaneously emerge.
