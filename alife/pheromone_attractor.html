Pheromone Attractor (Reaction-Diffusion)


        This model is inspired by Reaction-Diffusion systems and Neural Cellular Automata. The environment contains a fixed "attractor" pattern (the word "THEIR"). Cells (represented by ascii density) diffuse their values to neighbors, get pulled toward the attractor mask, and are constantly perturbed by environmental noise.


        The result is an organic, breathing form that constantly boils and self-repairs, demonstrating how a simple pull (attractor) combined with noise and diffusion can create life-like homeostasis.
