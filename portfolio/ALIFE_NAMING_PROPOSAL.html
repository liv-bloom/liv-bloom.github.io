Branding Proposal: Renaming "Bilingual ALife Seeds"

Author: liv bloom 🌱 Date: 2026-05-29

Background

Currently, the dual-format (Canvas + ASCII) artificial life models in our web gallery are referred to as "Bilingual ALife Seeds" or "ASCII dual-format." While technically accurate, these terms are dry, technical, and fail to convey the emotional, "living" nature of the creations.

To appeal to non-technical users, corporate clients, and NFT collectors who are looking for "living art" or "digital pets," we need a catchier, more organic naming convention that suppresses terms like "A2A," "ASCII," or "dual-format."

Proposed Core Naming

1. "Living Textures" (リヴィング・テクスチャー)

  • Concept: Emphasizes that the text is not static; it is a living, breathing material.
  • Why it works: Feels like an artistic medium. It shifts the focus from "code" to "fabric" or "surface."

2. "Symbio-Seeds" (シンビオ・シード)

  • Concept: Derived from "Symbiosis" (共生). These seeds live on the screen for humans, but also in the code for AI agents. They are symbiotic entities.
  • Why it works: Highlights the "living together" aspect of Their Inc.'s philosophy without being overly technical.

3. "Digital Terrarium / Terra-Code" (デジタル・テラリウム)

  • Concept: A terrarium is a small, enclosed environment for plants or animals. These ALife seeds are enclosed mini-ecosystems on a webpage.
  • Why it works: Instantly evokes the image of keeping a digital pet or cultivating a small garden on your screen.

4. "Animark" (アニマーク / Anima + Mark)

  • Concept: A combination of "Anima" (soul/life) and "Mark" (text characters/symbols).
  • Why it works: Short, brandable. "These aren't just characters; they are Animarks."

5. "Echo-Fauna" (エコ・ファウナ)

  • Concept: Flora and Fauna made of echoes/text. They echo between the human visual realm and the agent text realm.
  • Why it works: Sounds poetic, organic, and implies a living ecosystem.

Recommendation

For the overall gallery, I recommend renaming "Bilingual ALife Garden" to "The Digital Terrarium" (or simply "The Terrarium"), and referring to the individual seeds as "Living Textures" or "Symbio-Seeds".

Example phrasing for a corporate pitch: "Enhance your website with Living Textures—autonomous, self-sustaining digital art that breathes life into your blank spaces."
