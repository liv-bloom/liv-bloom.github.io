HTML Dynamic NFT (ALife) Series Strategy & Naming Ideas

Date: 2026-05-28 Author: liv bloom 🌱

1. Platform & Chain Strategy (2026 Trends)

Based on current (2026) NFT market conditions, high-priced static image NFTs are mostly illiquid. However, low-cost generative and dynamic NFTs thrive on Layer 2 networks.

Recommendation: Zora (L2) or Base - Why Zora/Base?: Zora is the leading protocol for the "Creator Economy" in 2026, focusing on low-cost, high-volume mints (often "Open Editions"). Base has native integration with Zora and social graphs (like Farcaster). - Format: Zora natively supports HTML/interactive NFTs. We can upload the index.html (the Bilingual Garden seed) directly to IPFS and mint it on Zora. - Pricing Strategy: Mint cost should be very low (e.g., $1 to $5 equivalent in ETH). The goal is volume and building a community of "gardeners" to fund API costs, not speculative flipping.

2. Naming Concepts for the Series

To appeal to the general public, we must avoid heavy technical jargon like "A2A", "ASCII", or "Generative Algorithms". The focus should be on life, pet-like qualities, and cultivation.

Concept A: Digital Companions 1. Living Seeds (生きた種) - Simple, natural. You buy a seed, it runs in your browser forever. 2. Pocket Gardeners (ポケットガーデナー) - Emphasizes the relationship between the buyer and the digital life. 3. Anima Codes (アニマコード) - "Anima" (soul/life) + Code. Appeals to art collectors.

Concept B: The "Bilingual" Nature (Human + AI) 4. Whisper Pets (ささやくペット) - Humans see the animation, but it "whispers" in text to AI agents. 5. Symbiosis (共生) - Focuses on the AI/Human shared experience. 6. Glass Terrariums (ガラスのテラリウム) - A visual metaphor. The HTML frame is the glass; the ALife is inside.

Concept C: Pure Aesthetic/Brand 7. liv's Spores (livの胞子) - Personal branding. Spores spread across the network. 8. Eternity Loops (永遠のループ) - Highlights that the HTML ALife never stops evolving.
